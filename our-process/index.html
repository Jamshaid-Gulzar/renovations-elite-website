Our Process

A Reconstruction Process Built Around a Clean Handoff

Property-loss reconstruction works best when the handoff is organized before construction begins. Our process is designed to establish what has been completed, what still needs to be rebuilt, what documents are available, and who needs to stay informed as the project moves forward.

1

Referral and Intake

We collect the referral source, property contact, loss type, affected areas, mitigation/remediation status, and available documents.

2

Reconstruction Review

We review the property's reconstruction needs and confirm whether the job is ready for planning, estimating, or additional information.

3

Scope, Selections, and Schedule

Construction scope, material selections, trade needs, access, and scheduling are organized before the active rebuild begins.

4

Reconstruction

The project moves through the required construction sequence. Progress and material changes are documented.

5

Walkthrough and Punch

Remaining construction items are identified, addressed, and prepared for final closeout.

6

Closeout and Partner Update

Completion is documented and the referral source receives the agreed project outcome/update.
