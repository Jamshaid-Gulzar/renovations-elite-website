Water-Loss Reconstruction After Mitigation

Water mitigation often requires removal of wet drywall, flooring, baseboard, insulation, cabinets, or other materials. After the affected areas are dry and the mitigation phase is complete, Renovations Elite LLC handles the reconstruction work needed to put those areas back together.

When We Step In

The Right Time for the Rebuild Phase

  • Emergency water source has been addressed by the appropriate party.
  • Extraction/drying work is complete or the project is ready for reconstruction planning.
  • Affected materials and rebuild boundaries are understood.
  • Available mitigation documentation, photos, estimate, or scope can be shared as appropriate.
Post-mitigation rebuild — dry interior moving back to finished condition
What We Rebuild

Putting Affected Areas Back Together

  • Drywall, ceilings, insulation, and wall assemblies
  • Flooring, underlayment, baseboard, shoe molding, and transitions
  • Cabinetry, counters, vanities, and affected finish components
  • Painting and finish carpentry
  • Trade work included in the reconstruction scope
  • Final punch-list and completion items

Renovations Elite LLC does not perform water extraction, structural drying, moisture monitoring, or emergency mitigation.
