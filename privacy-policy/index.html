Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026. This policy explains how renovationselitellc.com handles the information you share with us.

We collect the information you voluntarily submit through the project referral and partnership contact forms. This may include your name, company, phone number, email address, property details, project scope information, and any documents or photos you attach. We also collect basic, non-identifying usage data such as pages visited and referral source when analytics is enabled.

Information submitted through our forms is used to evaluate and respond to reconstruction referrals and partnership inquiries. We use contact details to follow up about the project or relationship you asked about. We do not sell your personal information.

Form submissions are transmitted to a form processing service and delivered to our business email. Documents and photos you attach are used only to evaluate the reconstruction referral you submitted and are not shared beyond the personnel involved in reviewing that project. Please do not upload sensitive information you are not authorized to share.

We may use a web analytics service to understand how visitors use the site. Where used, analytics is configured to avoid including sensitive claim or customer information in event labels. You can disable or clear cookies through your browser settings.

This website is served over HTTPS. We limit access to submitted information to personnel involved in reviewing the project or relationship. While we take reasonable precautions, no method of transmission over the internet is completely secure.

You may contact us to request access to, correction of, or deletion of personal information you submitted through this website. Contact Renovations Elite LLC at service@renovationselitellc.com or call 704-674-8783.
