Reconstruction Services

Reconstruction After Property Loss

Once mitigation, remediation, emergency stabilization, or contents work is complete, the property still needs to be rebuilt. Renovations Elite LLC coordinates the construction phase for affected residential areas, from framing and drywall through flooring, cabinetry, paint, trim, and final completion.

What we handle: the rebuild — not extraction, drying, mold remediation, contents cleaning, pack-out, storage, or emergency board-up.

Service Categories

What We Coordinate on the Reconstruction Phase

Service areaDescription
Framing and wall systemsRepair or replace framing, blocking, wall assemblies, insulation, drywall, and related materials included in the reconstruction scope.
CeilingsRebuild affected ceiling assemblies, textures/finishes where applicable, and associated trim.
FlooringCoordinate replacement of affected LVP, laminate, hardwood, tile, carpet, underlayment, shoe molding, and related transitions as required.
Cabinetry and countersCoordinate affected kitchen, bath, laundry, or built-in cabinetry and countertop work included in the project.
Paint and finish carpentryComplete paint, baseboard, casing, trim, doors, hardware, and finish work needed to restore the affected area.
Trade coordinationCoordinate plumbing, electrical, HVAC, and specialty-trade work that is part of the approved reconstruction scope.
Multi-room reconstructionSequence multiple rooms and trades under a single reconstruction plan when the loss affects a larger portion of the property.
Punch and closeoutComplete final walkthrough items, document completion, and close out the construction phase.
