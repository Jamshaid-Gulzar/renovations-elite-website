Fire-Loss Reconstruction After Cleanup and Stabilization

Fire and smoke losses can leave structural assemblies, wall systems, ceilings, flooring, cabinetry, and finishes requiring reconstruction after the emergency and cleaning phases are complete. Renovations Elite LLC coordinates the rebuild phase so the affected areas can move from damaged shell to completed construction.

Reconstruction May Include

The Rebuild After the Emergency Phase

  • Framing and damaged assemblies included in the reconstruction scope
  • Drywall and ceiling systems
  • Flooring and finish carpentry
  • Cabinetry and counters
  • Painting and finish restoration
  • Trade coordination
  • Multi-room sequencing and final completion
From stabilized, cleaned shell to completed construction

We do not provide emergency board-up, fire suppression, soot cleaning, smoke cleaning, odor treatment, contents cleaning, or pack-out services.
