Partner Commitments

How We Protect the Referral Relationship

  • We market ourselves as reconstruction-only on this website.
  • We do not solicit the referred customer for mitigation, drying, remediation, contents, pack-out, storage, or emergency board-up services.
  • We keep the referral source identified in the project record.
  • We provide agreed status updates rather than allowing the referral to disappear into a black box.
  • We document construction changes and project status in an organized manner, and close the loop at completion with the agreed final update.

Straightforward commitments. We do not claim exclusive partner status, guaranteed no-compete arrangements, guaranteed start dates, or guaranteed supplement approval. Arrangements are made per project based on agreed terms.

What to Send With a Referral

Everything Needed for a Clean Reconstruction Handoff

You do not need every item below to start the conversation. Send what is available and we will request the rest during review.

  • Property owner contact information
  • Property address or city/ZIP
  • Loss type and affected areas
  • Mitigation/remediation completion status
  • Available estimate or scope
  • Photos, 3D scan, or measurements if available
  • Carrier/claim information if the customer has authorized sharing
  • Known deadlines or access constraints
