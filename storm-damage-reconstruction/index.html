Storm-Damage Reconstruction After Emergency Stabilization

Wind, rain, impact, or storm losses can leave interior and exterior building components requiring reconstruction after the property has been secured and the emergency phase is complete. Renovations Elite LLC handles the construction phase for the affected areas included in the rebuild scope.

Potential Reconstruction Areas

The Build Phase After the Property Is Secured

  • Interior wall and ceiling assemblies
  • Flooring and trim
  • Doors, openings, and finish carpentry
  • Cabinetry and interior finishes
  • Exterior/interior repair coordination where included in the approved scope
  • Trade coordination and final completion
Affected areas moving from secured property to completed rebuild

Renovations Elite LLC does not provide emergency tarping, emergency board-up, water extraction, structural drying, or storm-response dispatch services.
